Methodology + Limitations

All data pulled from free public APIs: CoinGecko (price/mcap), DefiLlama (stablecoins), OKX (perp funding + OI), Frankfurter (FX → DXY proxy). No paid keys. No scraping. No tracking.

Composite signal is a simple weighted score across the four panels — not a recommendation. It tells you what the available signals collectively imply, nothing more.

Upgrade paths (require paid APIs or scraping): ETF flows (SoSoValue/Farside), MVRV + SOPR + LTH supply (Glassnode), exchange netflows (CryptoQuant), liquidation heatmaps (Coinglass), real yields (FRED — free but needs key).
